loki — queue worker for Node.js

A simple queue worker for Node.js that needs no Redis — just your database. Built for teams that want reliable background jobs without standing up another service.

  • Works with SQLite, PostgreSQL and MySQL out of the box.
  • Zero extra infrastructure — your existing database is the queue.
  • Written in TypeScript, ships ESM + CJS.

indonesian-date — date utilities

Indonesian date formatting & utilities — Senin, 16 Juli 2026, relative time, and WIB/WITA/WIT timezone handling. Zero dependencies, TypeScript-first, ESM + CJS.

  • Full Indonesian day/month names and relative-time helpers.
  • Timezone-aware formatting for the Indonesian archipelago.
  • Zero dependencies — small bundle, no surprises.
