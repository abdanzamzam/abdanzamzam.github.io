I build dependable backend systems that products quietly run on.

I'm Abdan — a Node.js developer based in Indonesia, working full stack with a backend-first mindset at PT. Astra Internasional, Tbk.

Since 2022 I've designed and shipped APIs, microservices and internal tooling that keep one of Indonesia's largest business groups moving. I care about clean TypeScript, well-modeled data, and APIs that are a joy to consume rather than a puzzle to debug.

Before Astra I built mobile and web applications as a senior app developer at Lexind. That mix — from the UI layer down to the database — is why I talk to product teams in outcomes, not just endpoints.

When I'm not in a terminal I'm usually reading about system design, exploring the JavaScript ecosystem, or sharing small packages on npm.
